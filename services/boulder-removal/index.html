Move the rock that will not move

Boulders can be far larger below ground than they appear at the surface. Safe removal depends on size, shape, depth, soil, slope, rigging options, machine access, and a clear destination for the rock.

Jim evaluates each project individually. Clear photos and measurements help determine whether the custom pulling system is appropriate or whether another method would be better.

Details that matter for boulder work

  • Visible length, width, and height with an object for scale
  • Whether the boulder is loose, partially buried, or deeply embedded
  • Distance to structures, pavement, utilities, septic components, and landscaping
  • Access width and where the boulder can be moved or loaded

Start with a complete photo set

Send close photos, wider site photos, approximate measurements, your city, and the narrowest access point. Include the desired outcome: moved elsewhere on the property, cleared from the immediate area, or removed from the site.

Boulder removal questions

Can every boulder be removed? +

No. Weight, depth, access, rigging, utilities, nearby improvements, and the destination all determine whether the work is feasible.

Can the boulder be relocated on the same property? +

Potentially. Include the intended destination in your photos so the entire route can be evaluated.
